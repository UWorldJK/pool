## Bugs found and fixed during QA
* Dialogs pinned left (Tailwind reset), choice rows overlapping (selector specificity), riders icon on its own line (SVG display block).
* Activity order within one transaction was arbitrary; now ordered by identity.
* Success feedback disappeared with its form; replaced by a persistent toast.
* Button label switched to "Saving…", which removed its accessible name mid action and caused a real race in e2e.
* Hero "Started at" showed the scheduled time, not the first pickup.
* All findings from `/code-review` (see `.10x/decisions/qa/core-ride-loop.md`).
* Pre-existing: `scripts/supabase-server.test.mjs` failed on Windows (raw path as ESM specifier).

## Gaps
* No test for concurrent claims (row locks are in place; a load test would prove them).
* No visual regression tests.
* Hosted Supabase not tested (out of scope).
